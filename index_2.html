About GlenJ Developments

GlenJ Developments is a trusted restoration and rebuild specialist serving homeowners across the region. With extensive experience in handling complex restoration projects, we bring damaged properties back to life with the highest standards of craftsmanship and professionalism.

Our Expertise

Fire Restoration

From smoke damage and structural repairs to complete rebuilds, we handle every aspect of fire restoration with care and precision. Your safety and satisfaction are our priorities.

Flood Recovery

We understand the urgency of flood damage. Our team works quickly to assess damage, manage drying, and restore your home to its pre-loss condition.

Full Rebuilds

Complete reconstruction projects handled professionally from foundation to finishing touches, restoring your home to its former glory and beyond.

Meet John

Founder & Managing Director

With over 30 years of experience in the construction and development industry, John founded GlenJ Developments to deliver exceptional restoration and rebuild projects. His commitment to quality craftsmanship and attention to detail has made the company a trusted name for homeowners across the region.

John's expertise spans fire restoration, flood recovery, and full property rebuilds — transforming damaged properties into beautiful, functional homes. He leads every project with a hands-on approach, ensuring that each job meets the highest standards from start to finish.

Outside of work, John is passionate about supporting his community and giving homes a second chance. When not overseeing projects, he enjoys spending time with his family and exploring the Lancashire countryside.
